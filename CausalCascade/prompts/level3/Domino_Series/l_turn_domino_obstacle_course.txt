On a gray tabletop in a corner room, two small yellow toy figures and a shiny gold ball sit on the left, a compact cluster of upright colored rectangular blocks stands in the middle with a red plank leaning against them, a purple cone is in front of the cluster, and a blue cylinder stands by itself on the right. The action begins when the red leaning plank slips and rotates down until it lies flat on the table. That loss of support makes the neighboring blue block tip over onto the red plank, which in turn destabilizes the rest of the central stack so the green and purple slabs topple and spread into a layered pile, while a tan slab starts to lean and settle across the fallen pieces. After the middle blocks have mostly collapsed, the taller black and white slabs on the left finally tip leftward and come down over the pile, creating a low slanted track. This leftward fall catches the nearby gold ball and sets it rolling along the fallen black-and-white pieces toward the center before it slows and comes to rest near the left edge of the collapsed heap. By the end, all the rectangular blocks are down in a crisscross pile around the purple cone, the blue cylinder on the right and the two yellow toy figures on the left never move, and the gold ball has shifted slightly and stopped beside the fallen structure.
